# Glossary

**Purpose** — give every term this library relies on a single short definition, so the
documents can use them without redefining them. Each entry links to the document that
explains it in full.

**When it applies** — whenever a term here is unclear, or you're writing a new document
and need to use one of these words the way everyone else does.

**Roles** — anyone may propose a change. If a definition here and a definition in a
linked document disagree, the linked document wins and this entry should be corrected.

## Terms

| Term | What it means | Defined in full |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Acceptance criteria** | The story-specific statements of what "correct" means for a particular story. Captured before work starts; satisfied before it is done. | [Definition of Ready](definition-of-ready.md) |
| **Default branch** | The branch a repository designates as its default — not always `main`. It always reflects what is in production. | [Version Control Usage](version-control-usage.md) |
| **Developer** | The person doing the work. Decides whether a story is ready, breaks it into tasks, and is responsible for getting it to done. | [Definition of Ready](definition-of-ready.md) |
| **Ideal day** | A day of work with no interruptions, no meetings, and everything you need already at hand. A measure of size, not of calendar time. | [Stories and Tasks](stories-and-tasks.md) |
| **Manual review** | A person's review and approval of a finished story. Required only when the story called for it at readiness. | [Definition of Ready](definition-of-ready.md) |
| **Requester** | The person who asked for the work. Owns what the story is for, and is its reviewer unless they name someone else. | [Definition of Ready](definition-of-ready.md) |
| **Reviewer** | Whoever approves the finished story. The requester by default; the requester may designate someone else when creating the story. Not every story has one. | [Definition of Ready](definition-of-ready.md) |
| **Story** | A unit of work that delivers something the requester values. One story is one Linear issue, and it is what carries an estimate, acceptance criteria, and a status. | [Stories and Tasks](stories-and-tasks.md) |
| **Task** | A step the developer identifies in order to deliver a story, tracked as a checklist item on that story. | [Stories and Tasks](stories-and-tasks.md) |
